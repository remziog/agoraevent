# Galata Tower — story film script (≈ 35 s)

Style: late afternoon light, stone and sea, drone-like moves, no people, no text.

| # | Dur. | Visual prompt (Veo) | Narration | Label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8 s | Aerial orbit around a round medieval stone tower with a conical roof above a hill of rooftops and water | Genoese merchants raised this tower in 1348 and called it the Tower of Christ — the highest point of their walled colony. | History |
| 2 | 7 s | Night, a lookout's lantern on the gallery, a distant glow of fire in the old city | For centuries it watched for fires across the Golden Horn — and burned several times itself. | History |
| 3 | 7 s | Time-lapse of the skyline from the gallery: Historic Peninsula, Bosphorus, the Asian shore | Walk the gallery once and the whole city turns around you. | History |
| 4 | 7 s | A figure-less pair of great fabric wings on the parapet, wind pulling at them, sea haze beyond | Around 1632, a traveller wrote, a man named Hezârfen strapped on wings of his own design and leapt. | Legend |
| 5 | 6 s | A glide across the water toward the far shore, shadow of wings over waves | He is said to have landed in Üsküdar, across the strait. No one else recorded it. Look at the view, and you understand why someone wanted to. | Legend |
